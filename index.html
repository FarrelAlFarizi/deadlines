Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use and has no ads.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. You sign in with Google, and the same account is what connects to Google Calendar.

What does the Calendar permission do?

It lets the app add, update and remove reminder events on your calendar. Google words this permission broadly, since it covers your events, but the app only changes the events it created itself. It is optional, and you can remove it at any time in your Google Account settings.

Why does Google show an “unverified app” warning?

The app is new and has not finished Google’s review yet. Signing in does not show the warning. It only appears when you connect Calendar, and you can continue through it by choosing Advanced.

Who can see my tasks?

Only you. Your tasks are stored under your own account and protected by security rules. You can export everything from the menu, and a one-tap delete button is on the way.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in any modern browser. Add it to your home screen for an app-style icon. Store apps may come later.
